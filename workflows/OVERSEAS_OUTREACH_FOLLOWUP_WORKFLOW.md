# Overseas Outreach Follow-Up Workflow

## Campaign rule
For every overseas prospect we send an initial outreach email to:

- Day 0: Initial email sent.
- 5 business days: First follow-up, in the same email thread.
- 10–12 business days: Final follow-up.
- After the final follow-up: stop chasing and move the prospect to nurture/watch status unless they reply.
- Always check replies, bounces and engagement before sending a chase.
- Do not automatically chase prospects with a reply, bounce, unsubscribe/negative response, or unclear status.

## Follow-up procedure
1. Check Gmail for replies/bounces.
2. Update Outreach Status and Reply in overseas-prospect-tracker.csv.
3. Filter for sent + no reply + 5 business days elapsed.
4. Draft/send a short personalized follow-up in the existing thread.
5. Record the follow-up date and next action.
6. At 10–12 business days, send the final short follow-up only if still appropriate.
7. Stop after the final follow-up and mark for nurture/watch.

## Operating principle
No mass chasing. Follow-ups are controlled by send date, response state and commercial fit.
