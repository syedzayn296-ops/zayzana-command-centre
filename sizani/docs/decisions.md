# Sizani — Decisions

## Product Definition

Sizani helps South Africans aged 50+ and the people who support them navigate everyday digital, family, health, care and government-support problems — by helping them understand the situation and find the right next step.

## Product Direction

Sizani combines two roles:

1. A 50+ digital life assistant.
2. A simple personal and family navigator.

The grant/support pathway remains important, but Sizani is not defined as a grant-only product.

## Five Core Areas

1. My Digital Life
2. My Family & Care
3. My Health
4. My Support
5. My Home & Safety

## Core User Journey

Tell Sizani what is happening ? understand the situation ? identify the right questions ? identify the appropriate pathway ? take the next step.

## Trust Principle

Sizani guides. Official services decide.

Sizani must not claim that a person definitely qualifies for a government benefit or service unless the relevant authoritative service determines eligibility.

## Development Principles

- Keep the interface simple and accessible for 50+ users.
- Prioritise clarity over feature volume.
- Verify information and official routes.
- Turn real user feedback into tracked improvements.
- Do not build features without a clear user or commercial reason.
- Avoid unnecessary redesigns.
- Preserve working functionality unless there is a justified reason to change it.

## Validation

The product should be validated with real people aged 50+ and with people who regularly support them.

Validation should determine which pathways and features deserve further investment.

## Zayzana Test

Sizani development should contribute to at least one of:

1. Making money.
2. Creating an owned reusable asset.
3. Making the next opportunity easier.

Ideally it should contribute to more than one.
